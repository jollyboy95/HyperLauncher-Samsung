## Weather data

Hyper Weather requests current weather and city search results from
[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/). Open-Meteo data is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Attribution is shown
on the widget and in Settings → About. No API key is required.
